Big Black Gym Log v0.9.93

Released: October 4, 2026
NEW FEATURES
Library: Added a Library page that tracks every book you read. Training books show the gains they actually gave you, other books are checked off as you finish them, and your reading now shows up on the calendar as post-its. Custom API keys need the Books log category added in Torn's API settings, or syncing will fail.

IMPROVEMENTS
Gave the calendar a full visual overhaul with new grid art, new post-it art, seasonal header banners, a redesigned weekly bar with glass tubes and metal brackets, neater post-it stacks, and easier-to-read day numbers.
Overhauled the EXP bar with a gunmetal finish, smoked glass, and a new crown for each atrophy tier. It now celebrates every level-up, opens a card with your rank, title, and progress when you click the crown, and pops up for new ranks, title words, atrophy tiers, and Fully Bricked, each with a button straight to your titles page.
Refined the titles page layout and rank plaques. Your title plaque now upgrades as you unlock more titles, and rank names on the scale are easier to hover and tap.
Greatly improved performance. The titles page uses far less CPU/GPU, the calendar, tooltips, and month changes are lighter, the panel snaps between sizes instantly, and images are around 45% smaller.
Added several quality-of-life touches: hover effects wait a moment before starting so sweeping across the calendar stays calm, page mode keeps its header in view while scrolling (Suggested by Svegarn), the stickerbook has a bigger grid, swiping back now suppresses Torn's Fly-Out Sidebar on the first swipe, and copied ledger and achievements text is cleaner.
BUG FIXES
Fixed war history from past factions never loading during Backfill. Existing API keys need the Faction log category added in Torn's API settings.
Fixed the calendar reopening on the last month viewed instead of today. (Found by Fishman)
Fixed several mobile issues: the grey tap flash, the off-center expanded panel, and near-misses on the expand button closing the panel.
Fixed several visual glitches: the blank Ecstasy OD post-it, the selected day's number shrinking in the expanded panel, and a misaligned Best Happy Jump row that showed duplicate totals for single-stat jumps.
Fixed the stickerbook briefly flashing the calendar on open.
Addressed the graph occasionally going blank when switching to Rates.

Big Black Gym Log v0.9.75

NEW FEATURES
Big Black Backfill: A powerful historical reconstruction engine that allows you to walk back and synchronize your training history, reaching all the way to the creation of your account.
