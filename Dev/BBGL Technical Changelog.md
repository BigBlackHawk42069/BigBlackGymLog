# Big Black Gym Log - Testing Phase Changelog

Version 0.9.93 - Pending

### New Features:
- **Library Page**: Added a Library view, opened from a new stacked-books icon in the SVG toolbar between Achievements and Stickerbook. Opening it grows the top panel down over the calendar, which stops painting and skips its re-renders while covered and is rebuilt once on the way out; page mode grows by the calendar's measured height and gives it back as a negative margin, so the page never changes height. At full height the glass overlay is sliced rather than stretched — only a band near the bottom with side glare stretches, so the top and bottom glare keep their normal proportions. Two pages with toolbar-docked pagination dots and arrows: the first lists the 16 training books grouped into Stat Books, Gym Gains, Energy, and Happy, each with a short description and a data column; the second is a two-column checklist of every other book. Every row takes an equal share of the height with no scrolling, and compact trims descriptions and abbreviates numbers. Unread books are greyed, books being read show a Reading marker, and finished books get a check. Changing pages plays the same CRT switch-off/on as the achievements pages, and pages can also be swiped.
- **Book Tracking & Training Book Gains**: Book use (2050) and book finish (2051) logs are now synced, backfilled, and kept through export/import, stored as item entries with the book's ID. From those, each training book's data is calculated from the log: stat books show the jump in their stat at the finish, measured between the gym trains either side of it (or today's live stat) less any stat enhancers used in between, flagged when it doesn't match the expected +5%; gym-gain books show only the extra gains their bonus produced over the reading window (`gain × bonus / (1 + bonus)` per train); energy and happy books show every gain made while they were active. Multi-stat books only show the stats actually trained plus a total. Memories And Mammaries takes the effect of the book read before it over its own 31 days (or pays a stat book out 31 days after use); when that's a training book it gets its own row at the bottom of the first page, tinted gold along with the book it repeated. Values from a book started before the log began are marked as possibly incomplete.
- **Book Post-Its**: Wired book tracking into the calendar grid, so your book activity now shows on the calendar on the day it happened, as post-its of four kinds. The day a book is used gets Training Book Started for the tracked training books or Perk Book Started for every other book, and the day it ends gets Book Perk Received for books that pay out on completion or Book Perk Ended for 31-day books running out. Every book gets exactly one start and one end, and an end still in the future doesn't show until it arrives. Memories And Mammaries takes both from the book it repeated, so it reads as whichever kind that book was.
- **Custom Notes**: Added a Custom Note button to the calendar header, centred above the month arrows (a blank post-it lettered in the same handwritten face as the first rank), so you can stick your own notes on the calendar. Every note goes through one editor: tapping the button opens it on today, and dragging the button onto a day opens it with that day already picked. The editor has month, day, and year dropdowns so a note can go on any date, and a full-size preview of the note; the text is limited to what actually fits on the note, checked against the preview as you type, with a 35-character cap behind it. The lettering is as large as the note allows without breaking words and only shrinks as the note fills up. One note per day. Picking a day that already has a note loads it for editing, with a Remove option; if you've already typed something, your text is kept and the editor warns that saving replaces that day's note. Notes can go on any day, past, current, or future. On a past week the note sits on top of the day's post-it stack, under a new-sticker post-it if there is one. With one other post-it on the day the two stack like any two-post-it day, the note in the lower spot; with more, the note sits centred in the lone post-it spot and the others spread out as though it were part of the stack. The note flies off first when the day is hovered or selected, with the rest of the stack following one step behind; it's never removed by clicking. A note just written stays put until its day is next left or clicked, so it doesn't fly away the moment it's saved on the selected day. The Clear Note label under it is faded while no day on the month shown has a note, and engages a clearing mode where every note is held in view with a red cross and clicking a day removes its note, until the button is clicked again (or Escape). Notes are saved in the log's history data, so they travel with export/import and are removed by Clear Data; demo mode notes are never saved.
- **Sticky Page Mode Header**: In page mode the title/settings bar and the top panel now stay in view while scrolling, so the grid can be scrolled without losing the top panel. Each rides in place until the top of the browser reaches it, then pins there — the title bar 8px below the browser edge, the top panel directly under it — while the grid scrolls underneath, and both release with the panel's bottom edge. The title bar is only filled (flat dark) while pinned, detected by an IntersectionObserver sentinel at its in-flow position, and stays transparent over Torn's own background otherwise; its height is tracked by a ResizeObserver so the top panel's offset follows the bar's font scaling. On the Sticker Book, which is shorter than the page area, the whole page container sticks instead, riding down until it meets the bottom of Torn's content column. The Library is left out and scrolls normally. Page mode's panel and body now clip overflow instead of hiding it, and any non-scrolling Torn wrapper above the panel with its own overflow is switched to clip while page mode is open, since either would otherwise capture the sticky and keep it from ever pinning.

### Bug Fixes:
- **Faction History Never Loading**: The Create API Key link never requested the Faction log category, so the faction application log Big Black Backfill reads to line up past factions' ranked wars was always refused and skipped silently. The key now requests it. Existing keys need the Faction log category added in Torn's API settings to pick this up.
- **Calendar Opening On a Stale Month Instead Of Today**: Fixed the calendar reopening on whatever month/year it was last left on (as old as January 2021 for one user) instead of the current date. The saved calendar position was only ever meant to seed the very first load, but it was also being read back in on every later load, so once you'd navigated away from the current month that position stuck for good across every future reload. The calendar now always resets to today's date on load; navigating still stays live-synced between simultaneously open tabs. Found by Fishman.
- **Mobile Tap Highlight**: Removed the browser's default gray/blue flash that briefly covered whatever you tapped on mobile — calendar cells, toggles, buttons, the sidebar and footer nav entries, all of it. Set once on the panel and each of BBGL's own elements injected outside it (the gym page EXP bar, sidebar/footer/flyout nav buttons), which is enough for every tappable child underneath to inherit it.
- **Ecstasy Overdose Post-It Missing**: An ecstasy overdose pushed a placeholder string instead of an image onto the day's post-it stack, so the post-it rendered as a broken, empty background. It now shows its own Ecstasy OD post-it.
- **Gold Bars Shine Misaligned**: The hover shine on gold bars only covered part of them. The bars were rotated 90 degrees in CSS, but the shine is masked by the same image and a mask can't be rotated without turning the shine's sweep with it, so the shine traced the unrotated bars and missed a strip down their left and bottom — about 9% of the bars never lit. The rotation is now built into the image itself and the shine's scale matches the bars', so the shine covers them exactly, with its sweep unchanged.
- **Expanded Panel Off-Center on Mobile**: Below about 590px wide the expanded panel sat 15px from the left edge of the screen but only 5px from the right. Its width reserved 10px on each side while its position used a 5px inset, and the two values were set in different places. Both now come from one constant, so the gaps match at every width.
- **Expand Button Closing the Panel**: The panel's expand/collapse button only responded exactly on its icon, and a click anywhere else on the header bar closes the panel — so a tap that just missed the button closed the panel instead of resizing it. The button now answers a few pixels past its icon on every side, without reaching the close button beside it.
- **Selected Day Number Shrinking in the Expanded Panel**: In the expanded panel, a selected day's number was meant to grow but its larger size never applied — the resting size rule was marked important and the selected one wasn't, so the number stayed its resting size inside a circle that grew around it, which read as the number shrinking. It now grows slightly when selected.
- **Happy Hopping Best-Jump Row**: Fixed the Best Happy Jump value sitting out of line with the other right-hand values, restored the missing colon after the row label, and reduced/baseline-aligned the compact date beside Best Jump. A jump trained in only one stat now shows that stat once instead of duplicating the same gain as both the stat and Total; Total is reserved for multi-stat jumps, with compact mode placing the stat label after its number.
- **Stickerbook Showing the Calendar on Open**: Opening the Stickerbook switched the top panel to the sticker book but left the calendar showing underneath for about a tenth of a second, until the sticker viewer replaced it on a separate 400ms timer. The viewer now opens in the same step the sticker book appears, from every way into it (the toolbar icon, returning from Settings, and reopening the panel on the Stickerbook).
- **Graph Going Blank on Rates**: Switching the graph to Rates could occasionally leave it empty until a refresh. The graph is built off-screen and attached at the end, so an error anywhere in building it (most likely a rate with no value reaching the Rates tooltip) left nothing on screen. A missing rate now shows as a dash in the tooltip, the historical rate lookup falls back on a missing value the same way it does on an empty one, and a failure while drawing the lines still shows everything built so far. Any draw failure is logged to the console with the mode, selected stats, view, and selected period, since the bug couldn't be reproduced.

### Improvements:
- **Weekly Bar Graphics Overhaul**: Redrew the weekly capsule bar to look like a physical, higher-fidelity piece of hardware. Each of the five bays is now a rounded glass tube with layered highlight and shadow bands, held in place by shaped metal brackets top and bottom instead of flat hatched rails, and the bays are joined by recessed metal couplings rather than sitting in one flat housing. The rails and end terminals were slimmed down so the tubes take up more of the bar, the bar's end caps pick up the same metal finish as the level bar, and empty bays show a dark tube recess. Completed weeks' glow was tightened to hug each tube instead of spilling into its neighbors, and the animated shine is clipped to the glass so it no longer runs over the brackets. The handle tab at the end of each bar now holds the week's summary chart in its own recessed inset; at rest the chart is scaled up to fill most of that inset, returning to its normal size when the bar is hovered or being viewed, and compact mode's raised tab was shortened to remove the empty space above the chart.
- **Bar Housing Recolor & Collar Detailing**: Recolored the weekly bar and level bar's cast-metal housing from an olive-brass tone to a neutral gunmetal gray, including the cast texture, bevel, and shoulder gradients and every matching shadow tint. Added a new recessed "collar" ring — a layered depth gradient plus a bright rim highlight — around the weekly bar's joint couplings and both bars' terminal end caps, giving them a distinct machined lip instead of a flat painted edge.
- **Level Bar Crown & Glass**: Replaced the single static crown badge with a set of four crown images, one per Atrophy tier plus a capstone for reaching Level 100 on the final tier, swapped in via a CSS variable keyed to the panel's Atrophy state instead of one fixed image. Each crown sits directly on the level bar's valve: it's lowered by its own measured amount so its base lands on the valve however much empty space its art has underneath, and its width is a fixed share of the valve's so it keeps the same proportion in compact, expanded, and page mode rather than running wider as the bar scales up. The crown and valve are anchored to the same point so they can't round to different pixels and land half a pixel apart. The level bar's glass also reads as smoked and reflective now, showing a blurred, masked slice of the header banner through it instead of an opaque flat panel — the header's own height and overlap were refactored onto CSS custom properties so it can resize smoothly per panel mode while staying correctly aligned with this reflection.
- **Level Bar Assembly Fit**: Shortened the cast end housings so they sit only slightly taller than the glass connectors instead of extending down to the weekday row, leaving an intentional gap beneath them while keeping the tube, valve, and housing visually locked together. The glass was raised by half a pixel and layered over the connector nipples so it reads as wrapping around them rather than disappearing behind them, then the complete assembly was lifted slightly off the weekday row; the gym-page copy receives one additional pixel of lift without changing the other modes.
- **Glass Layer Composition**: Moved the graph, Library, and achievements content beneath the shared glass overlay so every view reads as occupying the same physical enclosure instead of being painted on top of it. The glass remains at its original half opacity; the ranks/titles view gets a restrained contrast and saturation lift underneath it so its colors and illuminated details survive the haze without breaking the depth effect.
- **Titles Board & Rank Plaque Refinement**: Standardized the title drape's responsive proportions across compact, expanded, and page modes, including stronger narrowing clamps and a larger maximum-width expanded title, and moved the reset arrow into the plaque's upper-right corner. Rebuilt the illuminated rank plaque's top notch as the lower half of a rounded rectangle, centred and strengthened the RANK label within it, raised the rank name to a midpoint between its old arch-based and full-frame centring, and gave the frame, dark inset, and lettering physical bevel/depth. The frame and rank name now ignite together: the lettering remains visible as the same green-gray metal as the unlit border, flickers through partial illumination, then settles into a soft legible glow. The matching steel title on the progress scale uses the same face, relief, ignition, and final glow. Drape titles, plaque labels, and progress-scale rank names now scale independently by mode so compact stays readable while expanded/page layouts retain their intended proportions as they narrow.
- **Stickerbook Layout**: Gave the sticker grid more room. The page title strip under the grid was shortened by 3-4px in every panel mode, with the title moved down to match, so the grid band is taller (page mode's stickers grow into it when height is what's limiting them). In the expanded panel, the grid now spreads out into the arrow gutters as the panel narrows (none at full width, up to 75% of each gutter at the narrowest), so stickers shrink later and more slowly; only the outer columns reach the gutters, and their stickers sit in the top and bottom rows above and below the arrows. The page arrows are now centred on the gap between the two sticker rows instead of partway down the panel: they were positioned against a leftover 12px bottom offset rather than the space actually reserved for the title, which left them a few pixels below the grid's centre in compact and expanded modes.
- **API Key Scope**: The key's log categories are now defined once in the constants and shared by both Create API Key buttons. Dropped the Points category, since the points energy refill log it was requested for is already covered by Refills, and added Books for the book finish log. Book use and finish logs ride along on the existing sync and backfill calls that had room for them, so tracking books adds no extra requests. Existing custom keys need the Books log category added in Torn's API settings, or syncing will fail until they do.
- **Copy/Paste Output Refinement**: Reworked the clipboard text from the ledger and achievements pages for consistency. Every copy now leaves a blank line after its header, and the ledger copy's header reads Big Black Gym Log. Stat labels come before their numbers everywhere (Greatest Gains, streaks, Happy Jumps, enhancers). Lists that ran onto one line and wrapped awkwardly when pasted (streak gains, Best Happy Jump) now put each stat on its own line. Endocrine Enhancers spaces out its used entries, while copying the Happy Hopping title includes every Happy-item category even when its count is zero; overdose lines show both lost happy and energy. Empty values read `+0` for gains and `0` elsewhere instead of `—`. Layout copies were brought in line with their section versions: streak rows indent their gains, the Greatest Gains column copy labels each period, Best Happy Jump includes its date and time, and streak stat cells include the day count. Copying the Endocrine Enhancers title now flashes each copied row independently instead of replacing the complete two-column page with one oversized Copied message.
- **Toolbar Spacing for a Fifth Icon**: Tightened toolbar spacing so the new Library icon fits without resizing anything. Compact's icon gap dropped from 11px to 9px, the same floor the other modes use; page mode's smallest size (385px and below) uses an 8px inset and gap; and the expanded panel on screens under 380px scales its icons and gaps down together. All of these apply in every view, so the icons sit in the same place whichever page is open, and the extra clearance taken before the graph's stat pills is limited to the graph view.
- **Instant Panel Expand/Collapse**: The panel now snaps straight to its expanded or compact size instead of growing and shrinking into it. Nearly every size inside the panel is derived from the panel's own width, so the old width/height animation recalculated and re-laid-out the entire panel contents on every frame of the transition, which was especially heavy with the ranks page open. The per-element size transitions inside the panel (header height and padding, item viewer offset, ledger and header font sizes, flex/max-height changes, month header spacing) were removed along with it, so the contents switch sizes in the same instant as the panel.
- **Titles Page Layout Pass**: Reworked the measuring pass that sizes the titles page after it's built (star sizes, title card trim, rank bar placement, and the level-bar tooltip's title size). Every write now only lands when its value actually changed — a same-value write still invalidated the page's styles, forced the next measurement to recalculate the whole page, and could retrigger the pass's own resize watcher, including a variable written on the document root that invalidated all of Torn's page. Column measurements are now all read before any are written instead of alternating, which had forced a full recalculation per column; the rank bar's pixel-snapping now predicts its landing position from the current layout and writes once instead of writing, re-measuring, and writing again; and the resize watcher coalesces multiple notifications into a single pass per frame. Also removed the retired per-block SVG neon frame (long since replaced by the static plate outline and hidden) along with all the measuring and path-building the pass was still doing for it every time.
- **In-Place Title Picks**: Picking a title (clicking stars, the reset arrow, or the dev title override) now updates the star highlights and the identity card's title text in place instead of tearing down and rebuilding the entire titles page and re-running its full layout pass on every click. It falls back to the full rebuild whenever anything besides the pick itself has changed.
- **Rank Readout Animation Sync Scope**: When the live rank readout updates in place, the shared animation-timeline variable is now stamped only on the two parts it replaces (the rank labels and the card's rank badge) instead of on the whole achievements container, which restyled every element on the page and re-timed every running animation. A bricked-state or rank finish/material change, which switches animations on for elements outside those parts, still takes the container-wide stamp.
- **Calendar Shine Animations on the GPU**: Reworked the hover shine on gold, green, and diamond jewels and on stickers. Each shine animated its gradient's background position underneath an image mask and a blend mode, which forced the gradient to be redrawn, masked, and re-blended every frame for the full length of the animation. The gradient now sits on an inner band sized to cover the same travel and slides with a transform, so it's drawn once and only moved; the fades keep their original keyframes and easing on the outer element. The bands are kept to the minimum size their travel needs, since the whole band is rasterized when the shine starts.
- **Calendar Repaint Isolation**: Stopped hover effects on one part of the calendar from repainting the rest of it. The grid was one shared stacking context, so a layer appearing anywhere (a shine starting, a post-it peeling, a weekly bar sweeping) made the browser regroup and repaint everything drawn after it — later cells in the week, every week below, and the calendar content scrolled under the header. Each day cell and weekly bar track is now its own stacking context, each week (its row of cells plus its bar) is grouped into its own isolated container, and cells and weeks each sit on their own layer, so hovering only repaints what's actually under the pointer. The tooltip, header summary buttons, header month/year labels, month arrows, and header level bar received the same treatment against the header's own hover effects. Weekly bar sweeps now only hold a layer while their row is active rather than permanently.
- **Hover Intent**: Hover effects now wait for the pointer to rest on something for 60ms before starting — tooltips everywhere, the calendar cells' shine, post-it peel, and day-number highlight, and the weekly bar's handle, glow, and sweep. Sweeping the mouse quickly across the calendar crossed each cell in a few milliseconds and started all of that work for every cell it passed; it now starts none of it, while ordinary hovering behaves the same after a barely noticeable pause. A tooltip already on screen stays up while passing over other targets instead of flickering between neighbors.
- **Tooltip Rebuild & Measurement**: The tooltip no longer rebuilds its contents when asked to show the same content it's already holding, and remembers its measured size per tooltip so revisiting the same cell, bar, or graph point skips the two forced layout measurements it took to size and position itself. Cached sizes reset on window resize, web font loading, and changes to the level-bar tooltip's title size, and tooltips with images still loading are never cached.
- **Page Watchers Ignoring BBGL's Own Changes**: The two page-wide DOM watchers that detect Torn rebuilding its page (to keep BBGL's injected buttons and the chat-aware panel positioning intact) now skip batches of changes that happened entirely inside BBGL's own panel or tooltip. They were re-running their checks on every tooltip content swap and calendar/graph re-render. Anything Torn does, including removing or moving BBGL's injected elements, is still handled exactly as before.
- **Graph Hover Dot**: The graph's highlighted point dot now appears instantly instead of fading in over 0.1s. Each fade started a new animation on an SVG shape, which regrouped and repainted every line and point drawn after it, so dragging along a line repainted most of the graph on nearly every step.
- **Rank Name Shine Layers**: Animated rank names (emerald, gold, diamond) on the rank scale and identity card now sit on their own layer. Their shine is clipped to the letters and can't be moved to a transform, so it still redraws each frame, but now only the name itself redraws instead of the plaque grain, rivets, frame bands, and rank track under its glow.
- **Header Summary Button Alignment**: The All-Time, year, and month summary buttons beside the calendar header labels no longer shift away from their labels when hovered or active. The hover/active state used its own vertical offset for each panel mode (and a separate correction for the year row) instead of the resting one, so on hover the buttons dropped anywhere from half a pixel to five pixels depending on the row and mode — month happened to come closest, while year and All-Time visibly sank below their labels. Hover and active now keep the resting offset and only scale up from the button's bottom edge, so a button stays level with its label in every row and mode. The whole header label cluster was also nudged up 2px. In page mode, the year and month labels were lowered to sit on the bottom of their summary buttons, the gap between rows was tightened by 4px at every panel width, and the All-Time row was pulled a further 2px closer to the year row, with the month row staying anchored in place.
- **Cheaper Hover Selectors**: Replaced the calendar post-it peel's and level-bar tooltip's `:has()` selectors with plain classes set when the cell or tooltip is built, and narrowed the day-number circle's `transition: all` to the properties it actually animates, which stops every cell hover from starting an invisible transform transition.
- **Asset Format Conversion**: Converted the calendar reward/event icons, the sticker-frame and sticker-background art, and all 20 Gym/Casino stickerbook collectibles from PNG/JPEG to WebP, cutting their combined size by roughly 45% with no visible quality change — lossless WebP for anything with hard alpha edges (icons, stickers), lossy for the photographic backgrounds where it measurably shrank the file. The calendar grid backgrounds were replaced with new art altogether (see Calendar Grid Art).
- **Seasonal Header Images**: The calendar header banner now swaps between four seasonal photos (winter, spring, summer, fall) based on whichever month is currently being viewed, using standard meteorological season boundaries, instead of a single fixed image. Navigating across a season boundary slides the outgoing header out and the incoming one in, synced to the same animation, direction, and timing as the calendar grid's own month-change slide; stepping between months within the same season leaves it static. All four photos are cropped 1px off their right edge to hide a stitching seam, and the summer photo is additionally cropped 8px off its bottom edge to bring its sign down in line with the other three.
- **Swipe Conflict With Torn's Fly-Out Sidebar**: Torn's new Fly-Out Sidebar opens on a rightward swipe anywhere on the page, which collided with swiping right to go back a page on the calendar, stickerbook, achievements, and Library. Those areas now carry Torn's own `data-prevent-flyout-swipe` marker, which Torn's gesture detection respects, but only while the page on screen has a page to its left: on a first page, and everywhere else in the panel, the flyout opens exactly as it does anywhere on Torn. A right-swipe that goes back a page lifts the marker on that area for 800ms, so an immediate second swipe opens the flyout as if Torn had simply missed the first one; after that it returns only if the new page can still go back. The Library also gains the same left/right swipe paging as the stickerbook.
- **Calendar Grid Art**: Replaced all five calendar grid backgrounds (past, future, and the green, gold, and diamond reward tiers) with new higher-detail art, cropped to its tile edges so every row lines up with the cells, and encoded as WebP at full resolution. The grid art is dimmed by a translucent layer painted over the image but under the cells' contents, so stickers, post-its, and day numbers keep their full brightness. The green reward grid gets a green-only saturation filter that strengthens the green metal while leaving the rust its original color; to take a filter without it reaching the cell's contents, the reward grids now draw on their own layer beneath them.
- **Calendar Post-It Stacking**: Post-its on a day now spread across a fixed band of the cell instead of stepping down by a set amount, so any number of them stays inside the day — pairs sit further apart than before, and larger stacks tighten to fit. The bottom edge of the band is fixed, and the top lets the first post-it ride just above the cell. Event post-its were also made slightly smaller, and everything sitting on the pans (stickers, reward art, and post-its) was raised a little to fit the new art's flat faces. The green and gold reward art were enlarged into the extra room the new pans leave.
- **New Post-It Art**: New art for the Xanax OD, Ecstasy OD, War Lost, and new-sticker post-its.
- **Rank Scale Titles**: The rank titles on the scale at the bottom of the ranks page are much easier to hover and tap. Each one now answers across its whole section of the scale — a column as wide as its stretch of the bar, running from well above the title down past the bar — instead of only on the letters or lock glyph themselves, so tapping the bar underneath a title brings up its tooltip too. The columns sit side by side without overlapping, and the level marker keeps its own tooltip where it sits on the bar. Locked titles also show a larger lock. On the titles board, a locked star's tooltip now says what unlocks it — "Spend Energy training [stat] to unlock this title" — between Locked and the Energy progress.
- **Calendar Day Numbers**: Day numbers sit closer to the top-left corner of their cells in every mode, and every day's number now has a dark outline so it reads over the pan art — previously only past days had one. Other-month numbers are a little brighter. The shadow the weekly bar casts over the top of each week no longer darkens the numbers or their highlight circle: it's now drawn inside each cell beneath the number, while still shading the stickers, post-its, and reward art (the new-sticker post-it, which shares the number's layer, now clears it too). A selected day's number grows by less than before — barely at all in compact, and in page mode at narrower widths, which also uses a smaller circle; page mode at full width is unchanged, and the expanded panel's circles are slightly smaller.
- **Sticker Viewer Branding**: The branding printed on the back of each sticker is now sized as a share of the sticker itself rather than in fixed pixels, so it keeps the same proportion in every mode. It had been the same pixel size in compact and expanded despite compact's much smaller sticker, which left it oversized there. It's larger overall, holds steady in the expanded panel as it narrows, and in page mode scales down with the sticker at each of page mode's width steps.
- **Calendar Header Controls**: The month arrows, labels, and summary buttons now sit a fixed distance above the top of the level bar's tube in every panel mode, replacing separate per-mode spacing that didn't account for the tube — which moves them up. The summary buttons also get a dark outline and a slight brightness lift so they stand out against the header photo, kept underneath their glow on hover.
- **Month Swipe Cost**: Changing month (swipe or arrows) no longer doubles the calendar's layers while the old month slides out. Every day cell and week sits on its own layer for hover isolation, and the outgoing copy carried all of them, so the first frame of every month change rasterized the grid twice. The outgoing copy is a still image sliding away and now draws as one layer; the live grid keeps its per-cell layers. The seasonal header's image variables also moved from the panel root to the header itself and are only written when the season actually changes, where before every calendar render rewrote them on the root and restyled the entire panel.
- **Weekly Bar Rendering**: Each weekly bar is now drawn as an image of the same SVG instead of inline SVG. The bars' bloom and glow filters were re-run on the CPU every time the calendar painted (every render, month change, and hover near them); as an image they're rasterized once per bar state and reused. Looks identical, and the repeated gradient definitions no longer duplicate across the page.
- **Ranks Page Animations When Hidden**: The ranks page's animated plaque lighting and jewel glimmers are paused when you leave the page or close the panel, and pick up in sync when you come back. They kept animating unseen before.
- **Library Layout Pass**: The pass that fits the Library's labels, dates and stat cells now measures everything before changing anything, one step at a time, instead of alternating a change and a measurement per element, which forced a full page layout for each one. Multiple triggers in the same frame share a single pass.
- **Book Data Calculation**: Book gains are summed from the first train inside each book's window rather than by filtering the whole training log once or twice per book, and the calendar's book post-its are reused between renders until the book data changes or a pending end date arrives. Results are unchanged.
- **Page Watcher Consolidation**: The two page-wide watchers for Torn rebuilding its page are now one, so each batch of page changes is delivered and checked against BBGL's own activity once instead of twice.
- **Cleanup**: Removed unused functions and the stylesheet rules left behind by retired elements (the old level-bar backing, the Lv prefix, the pearl marquee bulbs, the suspended title sign and rods, the dual-column achievements layout, and a few others), about 300 lines in total. Shared helpers now replace repeated code for the month slide, calendar shine bands, top-panel view tracking, horizontal swipe paging, and local/TCT time formatting.
- **Starting Title**: The title shown before any stat has unlocked a word is now The Fresh Meat instead of The Untitled Man.
- **View Buttons Open the Right Place**: The View button on rank and title popups and the one on the level bar's tooltip card now share one route to the titles page.
